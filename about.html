About The Scent Plug

The Scent Plug is a small South African fragrance business. We sell affordable perfumes, roll-ons and body fragrances.

Our business focuses on providing customers with good smelling and long-lasting fragrances at affordable prices.

Our products are suitable for teenagers, students, young adults and working people.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide affordable fragrance products while giving customers a convenient and reliable shopping experience.

Our Vision

Our vision is to grow The Scent Plug from a small business into a recognised online fragrance retailer in South Africa.

Our Values

  • Affordability
  • Good customer service
  • Quality fragrances
  • Honesty
  • Customer satisfaction
