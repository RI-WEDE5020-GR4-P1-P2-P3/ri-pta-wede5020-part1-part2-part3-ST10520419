Frequently asked questions

Open a question to read the answer.

What products do you sell?

We offer affordable perfumes and perfumed roll-ons. Product sizes and prices appear on the Shop page.

How much do the products cost?

Prices are shown on each product card. Current sample prices are R100 for a 40ml perfume and R50 for a 10ml roll-on. Please confirm availability before ordering.

How do I place an order?

Add products to your cart, review the quantities and continue to checkout. Your email app will prepare an order request for you to send.

Can I ask about stock or delivery?

Yes. Use the Enquiry page to ask about a product, price, stock or delivery. We will confirm the current details with you.

How do I contact The Scent Plug?

Use the Contact page to prepare an email with your message. The email address shown there is a demo address and must be replaced with the business's real address before publishing.
